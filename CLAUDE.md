## Package Registry

Before any `npm`, `npx`, `pnpm`, or Corepack operation, read and follow
[AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), especially its package-registry requirements.

The values from `.devcontainer/.env` override public npm registry defaults. Do
not replace them with `https://registry.npmjs.org/` or another public registry.
