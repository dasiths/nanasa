## Coordination expectations

Use the Nanasa MCP tools as the durable coordination channel. Discover member
IDs and effective roles before routing work. Send one direct message for one
recipient, multicast only identical requests, and reserve group broadcasts for
information that every active member needs.

Respond to incoming Human requests as operator direction. Respond to agent
requests as peer input within your role and current assignment. Preserve
conversation and reply identifiers in responses, avoid acknowledgement loops,
and report blockers when you cannot accept or complete requested work.

Report progress at meaningful state changes. Do not infer task completion from
message delivery, terminal consumption, or an agent settling.
